# Itinerary Planning

Gather dates, arrival and departure cities, interests, budget and currency, party, pace, mobility, and dietary constraints only as needed. Treat user-confirmed bookings as fixed points. If dates or destinations are missing, give a provisional route and state what would change it.

1. Set city order and intercity legs first. Confirm exact airports and stations, door-to-door time, transfer buffers, hotel check-in, and departure-day margin.
2. Check opening days, reservations, holiday effects, weather-sensitive activities, and travel dates. Do not put a closed venue in the day plan.
3. Group nearby activities and include meals and rest. For children, older travelers, or wheelchair users, verify actual routes and facilities.
4. Mark bookings `confirmed`, `to book`, or `provisional`. Give reservation priorities and when each must be rechecked.
5. Explain cost estimates by item, currency, and source date. Keep unverified fares and prices as ranges or placeholders, not live quotes.

Output a compact city and transport overview, then a daily sequence at the level useful for the request. Include an alternate activity or route for a major closure, delay, or payment failure. Preserve Chinese names for stations, venues, and the accommodation address when known.
