# Entry and Documents

Entry permission depends on the individual route and is ultimately decided by the relevant authorities.

## Determine the route

Ask for travel date, citizenship, passport or travel-document type, purpose, arrival and departure ports, length of stay, and onward destination when relevant. Compare distinct routes:

| Route | Conditions to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Visa | Validity, entries, permitted stay, purpose, and document conditions on the issued visa. |
| Unilateral visa exemption | Passport nationality and type, permitted purpose and stay, policy validity, and entry conditions. |
| Mutual visa exemption | The specific bilateral agreement and document categories. |
| Visa-free transit | Eligible passport, genuine onward itinerary, permitted port and area, duration, and supporting tickets. |
| Regional arrangement | Geographic, group, port, or itinerary restrictions specific to that arrangement. |

Return `confirmed`, `not met`, and `unknown` conditions. Do not decide from nationality alone. If a decisive condition is unknown or official sources conflict, direct the traveler to the relevant Chinese embassy or consulate and NIA before travel.

## Official starting points

- [NIA English site](https://en.nia.gov.cn/) for current announcements and interpretations.
- [NIA visa-free transit](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html), [unilateral exemptions](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183390/content.html), and [mutual exemption Q&A](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183401/content.html). These are lookup paths, not permanent country or duration lists.
- [NIA arrival-card guidance](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c195170/content.html). Navigate to the current form from NIA if a direct form link changes; beware of fee-charging imitation sites.

Confirm that the passport used for a visa, tickets, and lodging matches the one carried. After a passport renewal, ask each issuer how to update its booking. Check current arrival-card procedure and the first destination address in Chinese. Hotel and non-hotel accommodation registration may differ; see [hotels-and-attractions.md](hotels-and-attractions.md).
