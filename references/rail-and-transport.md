# Rail and Local Transport

## Buying a train ticket

1. Identify exact departure and arrival stations in Chinese and English; large cities have multiple stations. Confirm date, train number, departure time, class, and passenger count.
2. Check current schedule, availability, document acceptance, identity verification, payment, and change or refund rules on [Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/index.html). Compare [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/) for an international-language channel, including service fees and support.
3. Enter the name and document type exactly as the selected channel requires and as shown on the travel passport. If rejected, use that operator's current guidance or support; do not guess a transliteration rule.
4. Before payment, review passenger, document type, stations, date, train, class, price, and terms. After payment, verify an issued ticket and save the order reference. "Booking in progress" is not an issued ticket.
5. Recheck boarding station, original document, identity checks, gate timing, and accessibility needs with the operator. Allow time for security and a large station.

For changes, refunds, missed trains, or an unissued third-party order, start with the channel that owns the order. Check the current deadline and rule. If departure is near, seek station staff or live issuer support rather than assuming a change is possible.

## Airport and station transfers

Compare metro or rail, licensed taxi, ride-hailing, and prebooked transfer against arrival time, luggage, mobility, connection, and payment readiness. Confirm the exact airport terminal or railway station and destination address in Chinese. Check current last-service times and pickup point. If mobile data or ride payment fails, use a staffed taxi queue or transit desk and show the Chinese address.

For children or accessibility needs, verify child-ticket rules, step-free routes, station assistance, and vehicle capacity with the responsible operator. A general claim that a station or ride is accessible does not establish suitability for one transfer.
