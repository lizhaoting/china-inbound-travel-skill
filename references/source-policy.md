# Evidence and Freshness

Use this policy for claims affecting entry, spending, bookings, or timed journeys. The repository's research register is discovery material, not a live policy database and not part of the packaged skill.

## Source order

1. Immigration and registration: China's National Immigration Administration (NIA), the relevant Chinese embassy or consulate, and the responsible local authority.
2. Tickets and terms: the actual operator or issuer, such as Railway 12306, an airline, hotel, venue, or payment provider.
3. Local transport and weather: current local operator or government information.
4. Practical friction: commercial guides and traveler reports can suggest what to check but cannot settle a legal rule or guarantee platform support.

Check the original page, not only a search snippet. Confirm publisher, publication or update date, geographic scope, and travel-date applicability. If a page redirects unexpectedly, returns an error, or asks for unofficial payment for a government form, start from the institution's homepage.

## Recheck per request

- Entry eligibility, permitted stay, transit route and ports, passport rules, arrival and accommodation procedures.
- Schedules, seats, fares, sale timing, changes, and refunds.
- Venue opening days, reservation windows, document acceptance, and holiday rules.
- Wallet and card support, limits, fees, phone requirements, app language, and ride availability.

For material conclusions, record a page title and URL, check date (and time for live fares, schedules, or availability), applicable citizenship and passport type or city and service, and any conflict. Use a concise source note in a quick answer.

If official sources disagree, explain the conflict and direct the traveler to the responsible authority or operator. If live access is unavailable, say so and give the institution or help route to check. Do not invent a current fare, seat, rule, address, or opening time.
