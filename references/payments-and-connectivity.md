# Payments, Connection, and Navigation

## Before arrival

Ask which cards and wallets the traveler already has, whether roaming or data service will work, and whether they have a Chinese phone number. App setup is a check, not a guarantee that a foreign card or number works everywhere. Prepare an independent way to pay and a way to reach the hotel without mobile data.

- Review current terms on [Alipay](https://www.alipay.com/) and [WeChat Pay](https://pay.weixin.qq.com/), including identity, card binding, transaction scope, limits, and fees where relevant.
- Check the issuing bank's travel controls and whether the intended merchant or transport service accepts that card or wallet.
- Compare roaming, local SIM, and eSIM for data and SMS needs. A data-only eSIM may not provide a Chinese number. Verify device compatibility and provider limitations.
- Save the hotel name, full Chinese address, booking contact, and first transfer offline. Test maps and translation while connected. [Amap](https://www.amap.com/) can resolve Chinese place names; verify its current language and offline features.

## When payment fails

For an immediate need, find another wallet or card, cash where accepted, a staffed counter, or another route. Then diagnose card binding, issuer block, merchant QR type, limit, network failure, or service eligibility. Never request a full card number or verification code. Check for pending charges before retrying.

For a failed metro QR payment, a staffed ticket desk or accepted alternative fare medium may be faster than wallet repair. Confirm current city options. For ride-hailing failure, use a staffed taxi queue or prebooked transfer. Without data, use the saved Chinese address and seek station, airport, or hotel staff assistance.
