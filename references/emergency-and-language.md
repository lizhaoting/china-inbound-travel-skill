# Urgent Help and Useful Chinese

If anyone is in immediate danger, lead with the local emergency action. In mainland China, call **110** for police, **119** for fire, and **120** for an ambulance. State the exact location and ask hotel, station, or nearby staff to help if the caller cannot communicate. [China government guidance](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025) lists these numbers; verify local instructions when possible. Do not delay urgent help to finish an itinerary or payment problem.

## Choose the immediate route

| Situation | First action | Independent fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical emergency | Call 120; give location, symptoms, and a callback number if available. | Ask nearby staff to call and guide responders; go to an emergency department only when safe to move. |
| Crime, assault, or unsafe location | Move to safety and call 110. | Ask hotel or station staff to contact police; preserve evidence when safe. |
| Fire | Leave the area and call 119 from a safe place. | Alert building staff and follow posted evacuation directions. |
| Lost passport | Report the loss to local police, then contact the traveler's embassy or consulate for replacement-document steps. | Ask hotel staff for the nearest police station and help contacting the embassy. Confirm replacement-document and exit requirements with the responsible authorities. |
| Missed or disrupted transport | Check the order's actual issuer and status; contact its support or a staffed counter. | Compare a later service or another mode only after confirming the first order's change and refund status. |
| No data, payment, or app access | Go to a staffed airport, station, hotel, or merchant counter; show the destination in Chinese. | Use an independent payment method or ask staff to call the property or operator. |

For a lost passport, do not promise that a police report alone permits rail, hotel, or exit travel. Check the current requirements of the embassy or consulate, National Immigration Administration, and each operator. The [Chinese government guide](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025) says to report passport loss to local police.

## Phrases to show staff

- **请帮我报警。** Please help me call the police.
- **请帮我叫救护车。** Please help me call an ambulance.
- **我需要去医院急诊。** I need to go to a hospital emergency department.
- **我的护照丢了。请帮我找最近的派出所。** I lost my passport. Please help me find the nearest police station.
- **我不会说中文。请帮我联系我的酒店。** I do not speak Chinese. Please help me contact my hotel.
- **请带我去这个地址。** Please take me to this address.

Show the actual Chinese address separately. Do not invent an address, phone number, embassy contact, or local service hours. For non-urgent cases, verify the responsible operator or authority's current help channel before giving a number or link.
