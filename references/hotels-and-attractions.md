# Lodging and Attractions

## Lodging

Before booking, confirm the property's Chinese address, arrival time, passport-name match, accepted payment, cancellation terms, and a contact route that works without a Chinese phone number. Check any claim about document handling with the property or booking channel; do not generalize from another hotel.

Hotels and non-hotel stays may have different accommodation-registration steps. For a home or other non-hotel stay, check current [NIA guidance](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c237273/content.html) and the local public-security authority's channel. If a hotel refuses check-in, ask for the exact reason, contact the property and booking issuer, preserve written confirmation, and seek local official help if registration or identity is the issue.

## Attractions and museums

Start with the venue-owned site or official reservation account reached from the venue homepage or a current local government listing. Verify opening day, ticket release window, passport acceptance, child and accessibility rules, on-site identity check, and refund terms for the intended date. Do not call a marketplace listing the official channel.

For several venues, check whether dates and travel time fit before starting orders. Present each venue, date, time slot, traveler count, document requirements, total cost, and cancellation terms for review before submission. If a portal will not accept a passport or lacks availability, use the venue inquiry channel or suggest an alternative; do not promise on-site admission.
