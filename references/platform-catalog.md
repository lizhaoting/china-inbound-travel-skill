# Platform Choice

Choose a platform for the task, not from a generic download list. These URLs identify operators; language, document support, fees, phone requirements, and foreign-card support must be checked for the current service and city.

| Task | Platform | Role and distinction | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rail | [Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/index.html) | Official railway channel; verify current passport workflow, payment, and ticket status. | Station counter or [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/). |
| Rail, flights, lodging | [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/) | International third-party channel; check issuer, fees, support language, and change terms. | Operator or property directly. |
| Chinese travel marketplace | [Ctrip](https://www.ctrip.com/) | Chinese-language platform related to Trip.com; interfaces and support may differ. | International Trip.com or operator. |
| Payment | [Alipay](https://www.alipay.com/), [WeChat Pay](https://pay.weixin.qq.com/) | Wallet and foreign-card terms vary by transaction. | Other wallet, accepted card, or cash where accepted. |
| Maps and rides | [Amap](https://www.amap.com/), [DiDi](https://www.didiglobal.com/) | Check Chinese place names, phone and payment needs, and pickup point. | Staff-assisted taxi, transit, or prebooked transfer. |
| Food and local services | [Meituan](https://www.meituan.com/), [Dianping](https://www.dianping.com/) | Often Chinese-first; check address, phone, and payment needs. | In-person purchase or property staff assistance. |
| Attractions | Venue-owned site or official account | Confirm the actual venue, passport support, release time, and refund terms. | Venue inquiry or alternative activity. |

State why a channel fits this traveler. When two are plausible, compare identity entry, language, payment, fees, support, and who can change or refund the order. Never present a community 12306 integration as official railway service.
